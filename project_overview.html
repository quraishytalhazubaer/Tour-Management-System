Project Overview

This document outlines a comprehensive tourism management platform designed to provide a seamless, transparent experience for tourists and a powerful, collaborative environment for internal staff. Explore the different sections to understand the system's architecture, features, and vision.

Engaging Tourist Portal

To create an intuitive public-facing portal for tourists to discover, book, and personalize travel packages with ease and confidence.

Streamlined Internal Operations

To centralize and automate tour planning, booking management, financial tracking, and staff collaboration for maximum efficiency.

Data-Driven Insights

To provide robust reporting and analytics, empowering management with actionable insights for strategic business growth.

Scalable & Secure Foundation

To build a secure, compliant, and maintainable system on a modern technology stack, ready for future expansion and new features.

Security & Compliance

Security is a top priority. The platform incorporates multiple layers of protection to safeguard user data, ensure system integrity, and comply with data privacy regulations.

🔑 RBAC

Strict Role-Based Access Control ensures users only access data and functions relevant to their role.

🔒 Data Encryption

All sensitive data is encrypted in transit (SSL/TLS) and at rest to protect privacy.

📱 2FA

Mandatory Two-Factor Authentication for all staff accounts to prevent unauthorized access.

⚖️ GDPR Compliant

Designed to comply with GDPR and local data privacy laws, respecting user data rights.

📝 Audit Trails

Comprehensive logging and audit trails for all critical system actions and data modifications.

💾 Backup & Recovery

Daily automated backups and a defined disaster recovery plan ensure business continuity.
